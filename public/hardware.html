Edge Pinout Allocations & Bus Architecture
3.3V Logic Safe
GPIO4 → DS18B20 1-Wire Data
Red to 3.3V, Black to GND, Yellow/Data to GPIO4 with 4.7 kΩ pull-up resistor to 3.3V rail.
GPIO14 → Magnetic Reed Contact
Configured as INPUT_PULLUP. Opposite leg to GND. Closed circuit = Door shut.
GPIO21 & 22 → SSD1306 OLED (I2C)
SDA to GPIO21, SCL to GPIO22. Powered at 3.3V. Default I2C address 0x3C.
GPIO18 → Active Failsafe Buzzer
Positive terminal to GPIO18, negative to GND. Sounds instantly upon out-of-spec excursion.
GPIO25 & 26 → Operational LEDs
Green on GPIO25 (Compliant). Red on GPIO26 (Excursion Alert). 220 Ω current-limiting resistors.
GPIO34 → Mains Supply ADC
Voltage divider drops 5.0V grid rail to ~2.5V into ADC. Analog value below 1000 flags power outage.
Production Edge Node Firmware (Arduino C++)

Compiled with ESP32 Core v2.x. Dependencies: OneWire, DallasTemperature, Adafruit_SSD1306, ArduinoJson.

"DOOR: UNSEALED" : "DOOR: CLOSED"); oled.setCursor(0, 44); oled.print(bad ? "ALERT: EXCURSION" : "STATUS: COMPLIANT"); oled.display(); // Send Telemetry Packet every 10 seconds if (millis() - lastSend > 10000) { lastSend = millis(); if (WiFi.status() == WL_CONNECTED) { StaticJsonDocument<256> d; d["temp"] = t; d["door"] = doorOpen; d["mains"] = mainsPresent; d["battery"] = mainsPresent ? 99 : 76; d["uptime"] = millis() / 1000; String body; serializeJson(d, body); HTTPClient http; http.begin(API_URL); http.addHeader("Content-Type", "application/json"); http.POST(body); http.end(); } } delay(500); }
Calibration & Regulatory Standards
WHO Good Distribution Practice

To maintain regulatory approval for critical biologics storage, deploy units according to standard operating procedures:

  • Probe Placement: Submerge the DS18B20 probe in a 100ml thermal buffer (glycerol vial or water bottle) to mimic internal drug temperature rather than fleeting air currents.
  • Door Barrier Calibration: Neodymium magnets must be positioned within 12mm of the reed switch to avoid false ajar triggers caused by refrigerator door vibration.
  • Periodic Calibration Check: Calibrate against a NIST-traceable reference thermometer annually at 0°C (ice bath) and +5°C (target baseline).
