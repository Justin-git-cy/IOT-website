Build Plan Verification Scenarios (Page 11)
Scenario 1 ✅

Normal Operation

Door closed, cold probe (+4.2°C), mains connected.

Expected: Level 'ok' · Cause 'Normal'
Scenario 2 🚪

Door Left Open

Temp crosses 8°C (+9.5°C) with door reed switch open.

Expected: Alert · 'Door left open'
Scenario 3 ⚡

Power Cut

Mains lost (mains: false), running on backup battery, temp > 8°C.

Expected: Alert · 'Power cut'
Scenario 4 ⚙️

Compressor Failure

Door shut, mains present, temperature rising steadily over 12 readings.

Expected: Alert · 'Possible compressor failure'
Scenario 5 ⏱️

Spoilage Clock Depletion

Hold temp > 8°C in demo mode until verdict reaches QUARANTINE.

Expected: SAFE → CAUTION → QUARANTINE
Scenario 6 🔒

Cryptographic Tamper Test

Edits old reading temperature in-place. Shows SHA-256 chain break live.

Expected: 'TAMPERED at record N'
