Diagnosis Rules (Your Wow Moment)

Not just an alert — an explanation.

Existing data loggers beep after vaccines are already ruined. Our firmware and backend diagnose the exact physical failure mechanism in seconds.

🚪

Door Left Open

Temp > 8°C + Door OPEN

Human error. Someone failed to close the latch. Local OLED and buzzer alert nursing staff immediately.

⚡

Power Cut

Temp > 8°C + Door CLOSED + Battery

Mains supply dropped. Voltage divider detects loss of 5V rail while backup battery keeps ESP32 running.

⚙️

Compressor Failure

Temp > 8°C + Closed + Rising 10+ min

The fridge is plugged in and shut, yet temperature climbs steadily over 12 readings (>1.0°C rise). Hardware fault.

🧊

Freezing Risk

Temp < 2.0°C

Vaccines can be destroyed by freezing just as easily as overheating. Alerts before liquid crystals crack vials.

📡

Device Offline

No packet for 60s

Heartbeat check flags lost connectivity immediately so dead batteries or Wi-Fi failures don't hide excursions.

Superstar Feature 1

The Spoilage Clock

A spoiled vaccine looks identical to a good one. Real vials carry heat-sensitive Vaccine Vial Monitor (VVM) labels. Our backend tracks total seconds spent above 8°C across any Wi-Fi drops (capped at 60s gaps) and gives a clear verdict:

SAFE — Less than 50% of the temperature excursion budget used.
CAUTION — 50% to 99% of thermal budget consumed. Secondary check recommended.
QUARANTINE (DO NOT USE) — Budget fully depleted. High risk of denaturation.

Superstar Feature 2

Tamper-Evident Hash Chain

Corrupt facilities sometimes alter temperature logs to hide spoilage. Every reading in our system includes a cryptographic SHA-256 hash incorporating the previous record's hash. Changing even a single past reading breaks every block thereafter.

// Hash Chaining Algorithm (Page 6 of Build Plan) const prev = chain.length ? chain[chain.length - 1].hash : 'GENESIS'; const record = { ...reading, prev, hash: sha256(prev + JSON.stringify(reading)) };
