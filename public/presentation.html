Viva Defense Questions

Viva Questions to Prepare (Direct from Page 12)

Click any card to reveal the exact model answer to practice with examiners.

Q1. Isn't a pharma data logger already available in the market?
Model Answer: Yes, but commercial units only record temperature data passively without explaining failure. Our system costs a fraction (₹1,500 vs ₹5,000–₹20,000), alerts in real time, and actively diagnoses the physical root cause (door left open, power cut, or compressor fault).
Q2. How accurate is the DS18B20 digital temperature sensor?
Model Answer: The DS18B20 has an accuracy of about ±0.5°C between -10°C and +85°C, which is completely acceptable for this working prototype. Real pharmaceutical regulatory compliance would deploy laboratory-calibrated probes.
Q3. What happens if the facility Wi-Fi drops or college network blocks the ESP32?
Model Answer: The ESP32 buffers readings to RAM (last 100) and writes counts to non-volatile flash. Most importantly, the local hardware alarm (OLED + active buzzer on GPIO18) operates autonomously without network. When Wi-Fi reconnects, buffered packets are posted in sequence.
Q4. Why not trigger the buzzer alarm from the cloud server?
Model Answer: Latency and cloud failure risk. A cloud outage or internet drop must never silence a medicine alarm. The local buzzer is the autonomous physical safety layer; the cloud web app is strictly an observational layer.
Q5. Does this IoT system claim to save lives?
Model Answer: No, we make no exaggerated medical claims. It significantly reduces the probability of unnoticed thermal degradation in cold-chain logistics and aids health workers in isolating refrigeration failures quickly.
